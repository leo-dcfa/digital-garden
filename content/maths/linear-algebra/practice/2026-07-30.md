## Express $|\psi\rangle = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ i \end{bmatrix}$ using Dirac notation

$$
= 1 \cdot \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + i \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = |0\rangle + i|1\rangle
$$

## 6.3) Compute $v^\dagger$ for $v = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ i \\ 2 + 3i \end{bmatrix}$

$$
v^\dagger = (v^*)^T = \langle v |
$$

$$
= \begin{bmatrix} (1)^* & (i)^* & (2 + 3i)^* \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -i & 2 - 3i \end{bmatrix}
$$

Reminder: conjugate is written as $(a + bi)^* = a - bi$, we flip the sign. A valid state must have $\|\psi\| = 1$.
